What Are Códigos Sagrados (Sacred Codes)?

Códigos Sagrados, or Sacred Numerical Codes, are a spiritual practice based on the belief that certain number sequences hold energetic vibrations that can help manifest desires, heal emotional wounds, and bring positive changes into one’s life. These codes are often associated with the teachings of José Gabriel Agesta, who claimed to receive them through divine guidance.

People use these codes for a variety of purposes, including:

  • Attracting abundance and prosperity
  • Healing physical and emotional issues
  • Finding love or improving relationships
  • Achieving personal goals (such as selling a house, getting a job, or quitting bad habits)

How to Use Códigos Sagrados

Choose the Right Code

Each numerical sequence corresponds to a specific purpose. For example:

42916 – Sell a house quickly

71588 – Attract financial abundance

3333 – Angelic protection

25543 – Quit smoking

Repeat the Code 45 Times

The standard practice is to repeat the number sequence 45 times in a row while focusing on your intention.

  • Some people count on their fingers, use a rosary, or write it down repeatedly.

Set a Clear Intention

Before reciting the code, take a deep breath and mentally or verbally state your intention. Example:

"I activate the sacred code 42916 to sell my house quickly, easily, and at the best price."

Use Visualization and Emotion

  • Imagine the outcome you desire as if it has already happened.
  • Feel gratitude and excitement as if the manifestation is already real.

Practice Daily (Optional)

  • Some people repeat the code for at least 21 days to strengthen the energy.
  • Others use it as needed or until they see results.
